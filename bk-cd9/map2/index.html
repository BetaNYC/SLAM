About this map

A second layout of SLAM's map of Brooklyn Community District 9, after BetaNYC's map of Manhattan's privately owned public spaces. Each layer has its own on/off button. On the map, outdoor dining is drawn over cannabis, and cannabis over liquor. The years apply to 311 complaints only.

Data: SLAM's nightly files for this district, from State Liquor Authority licenses and applications (data.ny.gov f8i8-k2gm, 9s3h-dpkz, 6dg3-2z7i), the Office of Cannabis Management (gttd-5u6y, jskf-tt3q), Dining Out NYC (fpeh-f7ci), 311 Service Requests (erm2-nwe9), the Facilities Database (ji82-xba5) and PLUTO (64uk-42ks). BetaNYC placed and grouped the records, so this is not an official City or State record. Updated Nov 24, 2026, 3:22 PM.
