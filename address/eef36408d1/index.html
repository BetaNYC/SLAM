Schools and houses of worship nearby

Distances are approximate. The law measures from the center of the nearest entrance of one building to the center of the nearest entrance of the other. SLAM cannot measure door to door: it has no entrance locations. It measures a straight line between approximate building points, from the City's address search or from each dataset's own map point. Those points can sit away from the door, so a SLAM distance can differ from an entrance-to-entrance measurement by tens of feet or more, especially for large or corner buildings. Treat a distance near a limit as close, not as over or under it.

No schools or houses of worship in the data within about 200 ft of this address.

"Other religious property" is a tax lot in PLUTO building class M2, M3, M4 or M9 (mission house, parsonage or rectory, convent, miscellaneous). PLUTO does not say it is a place of worship.

The rules these distances relate to

New York Alcoholic Beverage Control Law §64(7) sets a 200-foot rule about schools and places of worship and a 500-foot rule about three or more existing on-premises licensed premises, measured entrance to entrance. SLAM measures building point to building point, so every distance here is approximate. This page does not decide whether a rule applies; the State Liquor Authority (liquor) or the Cannabis Control Board (cannabis) makes that decision.
