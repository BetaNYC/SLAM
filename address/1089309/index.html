"Show on the SLA's LAMP map" opens SLA's LAMP map centered on the City's address point for the building when SLAM matched the address, otherwise on the point in SLA's open data, at zoom 18. (The open data's points come from data.ny.gov's own geocoding and can be blocks off for Queens addresses written without the hyphen; LAMP's own pins were within about 200 ft of the City's point when we checked on 2026-11-05.) It opens a map location, not the license record. LAMP first shows a welcome box; close it. LAMP places each license at its own point, which can differ from the link's center. Against SLA's open-data points (2026-11-04), most LAMP pins were within about 13 m, but 145 of 2,344 were more than 50 m away; against the City's address point we have checked only four Queens licenses (2026-11-05), whose LAMP pins were 92 to 203 ft away. LAMP shows no pin for "Additional Bar" permits. Check that the pin you click has the same license ID. When we checked (2026-11-04), SLA's LAMP popup listed premises and license details, principals and disciplinary history, and several licenses at one spot are paged ("1 of 6"). The point is the dataset's georeference, which the data.ny.gov platform geocodes from the address ("Open Data platform-generated geocoding information from supplied address components. Point-type location is the centroid of the address components provided and does not reflect a specific address if the street address component is not provided."). It is not SLAM's geocode. A license with no point has no link.

Hours and method of operation. The SLA open data does not include method of operation or hours, and neither did the LAMP popup when we checked. Approval conditions for contested applications, which can include closing hours and community board stipulations, appear in the minutes of SLA's Full Board meetings; each meeting's page in the SLA event calendar links its minutes once they are posted after the meeting.

Schools and houses of worship nearby

Distances are approximate. The law measures from the center of the nearest entrance of one building to the center of the nearest entrance of the other. SLAM cannot measure door to door: it has no entrance locations. It measures a straight line between approximate building points, from the City's address search or from each dataset's own map point. Those points can sit away from the door, so a SLAM distance can differ from an entrance-to-entrance measurement by tens of feet or more, especially for large or corner buildings. Treat a distance near a limit as close, not as over or under it.

No schools or houses of worship in the data within about 200 ft of this address.

"Other religious property" is a tax lot in PLUTO building class M2, M3, M4 or M9 (mission house, parsonage or rectory, convent, miscellaneous). PLUTO does not say it is a place of worship.
