Help check the State's cannabis records

BetaNYC is turning New York State's Cannabis Control Board documents into open data. A computer read the State's PDFs. Some readings need a person to look. You will see a small picture of a page from the State's PDF and what the computer read from it. Tell us whether they match.

How to check an item

  • Matches: the picture inside the red box shows exactly what we read. Every letter, number and word is the same. Capital letters and punctuation count.
  • Doesn't match: anything is different: a letter, a number, a missing or extra word. If you can, type what the picture shows.
  • Can't tell: the picture is unclear, cut off, or the red box is around the wrong thing.

Examples

  • We read "Adult-Use Cul@vator License" and the picture shows "Adult-Use Cultivator License". That doesn't match. Type "Adult-Use Cultivator License".
  • We read "Business address: (none found)" and the letter gives an address. That doesn't match. Type the address the letter gives.
  • A word breaks onto a second line inside the box. That is fine; it can still match.
