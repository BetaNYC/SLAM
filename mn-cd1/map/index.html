About this map

311 complaints are off by default because they are dense. They are colored by type, based on the first SLAM's layers: commercial noise, drinking, outdoor dining, and other. "Stacked chips" piles the complaints at one point into a column, as the first SLAM did, so busy addresses stand out; a stack stops at 12 chips and shows the total above it. "Circles sized by count" draws one circle per point, larger for more complaints, colored by the most common type, with the count inside. Selecting any point shows its details in a panel beside the map (under it on a phone), with a link to its full address page. The map keeps your view, layers and selection in its web address, so Back returns you here; your layer choices are also remembered in this browser for next time. Schools (teal), houses of worship (dark pink) and other religious property (violet) are off by default; selecting one shows its name, type and address in the panel. Schools come from the City's Facilities Database; houses of worship are PLUTO tax lots in building class M1, and other religious property is class M2, M3, M4 or M9 (PLUTO has no names). Street events have no coordinates in the source and are not on the map. The street map is the City's own basemap, from NYC's Office of Technology and Innovation; if it does not load, or stops loading while you use the map, OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap data) is used instead. The list view is the accessible equivalent of this map: every license, cannabis, Dining Out, school and religious-property point is in it, and its 311 tab counts every complaint and lists the most recent ones.
